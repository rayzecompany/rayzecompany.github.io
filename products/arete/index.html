Arete

iOS · Beta

A workout log for iPhone that tells you how recovered you are and what to train next.

Log your sets as you normally would. Arete turns them into a freshness score for each muscle group and a read on whole-body fatigue, so you can see at a glance what's ready to train today. Log RPE or RIR if you track effort, and Arete factors in how hard each set was.

Everything stays on your iPhone. There's no account and no network calls, so your training data never leaves your phone.
