Pure Poker Simulator

Mac · Windows

Texas Hold'em against characters from six TV comedies, with an optional coach.

The opponents play from real poker strategy, and the coach walks you through the same math as you play: pot odds, how often to defend, and when a bluff pays off.

The deal is fair. Every hand comes from a shuffled 52-card deck using cryptographic randomness, your cards are dealt by the same code as everyone else's, and there's no difficulty setting or rigging. It's free for Mac and Windows.
